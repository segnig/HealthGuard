Welcome to HealthGuard

HealthGuard helps you build healthier digital habits with two core systems: screen time management that tracks usage and prompts restorative breaks, and sound protection that monitors loudness and helps protect your hearing.

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

The 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
